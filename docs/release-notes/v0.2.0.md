A new, simpler layout, a panel right at the tray icon, one-click "fastest server", and Persian.

If you have Raylit 0.1.0, it updates itself: on Windows and Linux, wait for **Restart to update** at the top of the
window (or choose **Check for updates** in Settings → Updates).

### Downloads

- **Windows:** `Raylit-Setup-0.2.0.exe`
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Raylit-0.2.0-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Linux:** `Raylit-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage`

Each download includes [Xray-core](https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core) (MPL-2.0, unmodified).

### New

- **A page for each job.** An icon bar on the side leads to Connection, Servers, Subscriptions, Log and Settings. The
  Connection page holds just the big button, the server you use, the speed graph and the two switches that matter
  (proxy mode and routing). A green dot on the Connection icon shows you are connected from any page.
- **Servers as cards** with a country badge (from the flag in the server's name), the protocol and a coloured delay.
  Click a card to choose it, double-click to connect, **⋯** for edit, share and delete.
- **Subscriptions page** with data used, days left, last update and an Update button per subscription.
- **Tray panel.** Click the tray icon: a small panel opens next to it to connect, switch servers or change the proxy
  mode without opening the window. Right-click for the menu.
- **Connect to the fastest server.** One click tests your servers and connects to the quickest that works.
- **فارسی.** Settings → General → Language: the whole app in Persian, right to left, with the Vazirmatn font and the
  Persian calendar. English stays the default.

### Fixes

- Running two delay tests at once no longer starts two sets of test cores; the second waits for the first.

The builds are not code-signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. On
macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
